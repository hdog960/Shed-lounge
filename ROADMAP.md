# Renovation Roadmap
Last planned: 2026-11-10. Checkboxes are **not** completed work unless actually verified.

## Gate 0 — Field survey (ACTIVE)
- [ ] Confirm jurisdiction (city/county) and any permits needed for wiring/occupancy changes
- [ ] Locate house breaker panel and assess electrician access; no panel cover removal required
- [ ] Measure practical cable route along the ground from house to shed
- [ ] Determine shed structural classification, foundation/support and whether relocation is possible
- [ ] Check roof/floor/door for leaks, dampness, pest trails, daylight-through-gaps
- [ ] Confirm desired electrical loads now and for possible future heating/cooling/ventilation

**Gate complete when:** safe route options and scope are known, not when work is installed.

## Gate 1 — Electricity (TOP PRIORITY)
- [ ] Get licensed electrician assessment and written quote for permanent supply
- [ ] Confirm maximum permitted circuit size, local code, GFCI, disconnect, grounding, permits and inspections
- [ ] Request underground utility marking before digging; do not presume clear ground
- [ ] Design outlets around existing shelves, mini-fridge and accessible switch location
- [ ] Plan a sufficiently bright switch-operated ceiling LED, preferably dimmable and suitable for environment
- [ ] Obtain any required permits, install and inspect
- [ ] Verify switch/outlets function properly

**Gate complete when:** installation is inspected/approved where required and usable.

## Gate 2 — Bug resistance (TOP PRIORITY)
- [ ] Inspect/photograph all likely insect entry points on a dry day and at dusk
- [ ] Repair water leaks and address dampness first
- [ ] Weatherstrip door jambs and install appropriate threshold and door sweep
- [ ] Seal wall-to-floor perimeter, corners and other unintended openings with suitable material
- [ ] Screen legitimate ventilation openings with suitable insect mesh; **do not obstruct required airflow**
- [ ] Remove debris and maintain drainage/clearance around exterior
- [ ] Recheck after rainfall and at night for infiltration

**Gate complete when:** pest entry is substantially reduced and the shed stays dry/ventilated. Completely bug-free cannot be guaranteed.

## Gate 3 — Comfort and safety
- [ ] Decide fresh-air intake/exhaust, especially if anyone will smoke inside
- [ ] Plan safe temperature/humidity management and evaluate insulation compatibility
- [ ] Evaluate smoke alarm and safe exits; no open flames near wood/decor
- [ ] Check surface prep, weather protection and flooring needs

## Gate 4 — Furniture and function
- [ ] Measure *clear* seating/table floor space once shelves are measured
- [ ] Fit a proper large table while keeping usable door egress
- [ ] Select compact seating for two and functional storage
- [ ] Confirm accessible fridge and beverage/snack placement

## Gate 5 — Decor (LOCKED until infrastructure is ready)
- [ ] One strong overhead light, coordinated low-key accents only
- [ ] Cozy fantasy / game-inspired theme, balanced for both people
- [ ] Some whimsical/cozy favorites and personal crafts/books
- [ ] Final space/comfort inspection

## Updates
Record completed tasks and dated proof in GitHub Issues, commits or the project journal. Never mark an electrical task completed based on assumptions.
