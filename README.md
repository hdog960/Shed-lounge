# Shed Lounge — Renovation Headquarters

A long-term, real-world renovation of a small existing wooden shed into a shared, cozy **stoner × gamer-themed lounge**. This public repository tracks the plan, decisions, budget and work completed. **Plans are not evidence of completed construction.**

**Status (2026-11-10): PLANNING. No electrical work or bug-sealing verified complete.**

## Starting conditions
- Interior floor area excluding fixed shelves: **114 in long × 77 in wide** (9 ft 6 in × 6 ft 5 in), about **60.96 sq ft**
- Barn/gambrel-shaped roof, exposed OSB and timber ribs
- Existing built-in shelves on both sides **must remain fixed**
- Existing door at one end; currently no confirmed safe electrical service to shed
- House has a crawl space and an unfinished attic; no outdoor outlet has been identified
- Exact house-to-shed distance, foundation type, shed electrical classification and local permitting jurisdiction are **not yet verified**

## Priorities
1. **Electricity** — safe professional electrical design, permitting and installation
2. **Bug resistance** — inspect and close entry points without sealing required ventilation
3. Climate/moisture and smoke ventilation
4. Finishes, furnishings and decor

## Locked design preferences
- Overall style: **50% gamer-inspired fantasy/cozy worldbuilding, 25% stoner-lounge, 25% personal touches**
- Shared feel; represent both partners fairly
- **No TV or gaming station**; themed lounge only
- **One bright, dimmable, switch-operated overhead light**; avoid numerous small decorative lights
- Bigger, useful central table; no plants necessary
- Preserve original fixed shelving and realistic clearances
- Cozy dark fantasy + charming whimsical/cottage-inspired touches, not a merchandise wall
