# Electrical — Planning Notes (NOT wiring instructions)
Updated: 2026-11-10. **Status: no install confirmed.**

## What we know
- Shed is detached from house across an open yard.
- House reportedly has a crawl space and an unfinished attic.
- No exterior outlet identified yet.
- The existing roof/wall framing is wood/OSB.
- Desired use: bright overhead light on one switch, 3–4 convenient receptacles if permitted, charging, small fridge, possibly ventilation/climate control.

## Recommended first choice to price
Get a qualified electrician to evaluate a **permanent underground supply** from the existing house electrical panel, potentially via the crawl space if it provides a safe accessible route. This is a *concept*, not an approved route.

Aerial supply, new exterior conduit paths or a different approved routing may be worth comparing. Do not assume the house attic is needed or safe to traverse.

## Important Indiana classification question
Indiana Residential Code rules for certain **portable accessory structures 200 sq ft or smaller** may limit supply to **one 20-amp circuit** and restrict permanent heating. Whether this specific shed counts as portable, accessory, habitable or another classification, and whether its planned use changes requirements, must be verified with the local authority/contractor first.

Reference: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/indiana/675-IAC-14-4.4-12 and official Indiana code listing https://www.in.gov/dhs/boards-and-commissions/fpbsc-rules/

## Questions for electrician
1. Where is the house electrical panel, is there capacity and a safe source point?
2. Can the wiring route through an accessible crawl space? What is the realistic run length?
3. What is the shed's legal classification and allowed circuit/feeder configuration?
4. What permits/inspections apply at this specific address (NOT posted publicly)?
5. What burial method/depth and 811 utility markings are required for the route?
6. Where should a disconnect, any grounding/bonding provisions and GFCI protection go?
7. How many receptacles are sensible once fixed shelves and large table are measured?
8. Can later smoke exhaust, air conditioning or heating be accommodated safely and legally?
9. What total installed price covers trenching, materials, labor and inspections?

## Safety boundary
- Do not run an extension cord through the yard, door, wall, floor, attic or under a rug as permanent power.
- Do not attempt panel wiring, underground electrical construction or work in an unsafe attic.
- Locate underground utilities before any digging.
- Keep ventilation and smoke management separate from simply adding electrical power.
